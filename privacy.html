Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy covers Animystic Autopost, an internal tool run by Animystic (the "channel", "we") to publish our own content to our own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

Who uses the tool

Only the Animystic team. The tool is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data the tool accesses

  • From TikTok (after we sign in with our own TikTok account and approve access): our account's basic profile information (account ID and display name) and permission to upload videos to our own account.
  • From Meta (Instagram and Facebook): access to post to Animystic's own Facebook Page and Instagram account.

The tool does not read, collect or store data about any other TikTok, Instagram or Facebook user: no followers, comments, messages, contacts or viewing data.

How the data is used

Only to upload and publish Animystic's own videos and images, and to confirm that an upload succeeded.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the Animystic team's own computer, in the operating system's user settings. They are never sold, shared or sent to third parties. The content we post is our own work.

How long we keep it

Tokens are kept until they expire or until we disconnect the app. You can disconnect Animystic Autopost at any time in your TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) or in your Facebook/Instagram settings, which stops all access immediately.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
