ANIMYSTIC

Short claymation stories about animals, nature and history

Animystic is a small creator channel on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok (@animystic.motion). We make short stop-motion-style stories: one surprising fact, told in a few slides or a short video.

Animystic Autopost is our own internal tool. It uploads videos and images that Animystic has made to Animystic's own social media accounts, so we can schedule our posts. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account or data.
