Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Animystic Autopost is an internal tool that publishes Animystic's own content to Animystic's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and TikTok account. It does not collect or store data about any other user.

What the tool keeps

Only the access tokens for Animystic's own accounts, stored on the Animystic team's computer. No data about visitors, followers or other people is collected.

How to remove access and delete data

  1. Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → remove the app. This revokes its access immediately.
  2. Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → remove the app.
  3. TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions → remove the app.

Once access is removed, the stored token no longer works and is deleted from our computer.

Request deletion by email

To ask us to delete anything related to your account, email chhetri.bibek@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We confirm within 30 days.
